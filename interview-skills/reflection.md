## 🎯 Reflection (STAR Technique)

**SITUATION:**  
As I prepared for internship and junior developer interviews, I recognized the importance of combining technical readiness with strong communication skills. I wanted to ensure I could clearly present my achievements, from CRM workflows to Power BI analytics, in a confident and structured way.

**TASK:**  
My goal was to strengthen my interview performance through three focus areas:  
1. Theoretical understanding of interview principles.  
2. Practicing structured responses to technical and behavioral questions.  
3. Performing targeted research on potential employers to tailor my approach.

**ACTION:**  
- Completed the **Interview Skills Assessment** to consolidate theory.  
- Practiced technical questions using STAR responses drawn from my experience at **DataBalk** and **Take n Tora**.  
- Integrated my **PL-300** and **AI-900** certification projects into my answers to showcase applied learning.  
- Researched company cultures and technologies to prepare thoughtful, personalized questions.

**RESULT:**  
- Gained confidence in articulating both technical and soft skills.  
- Successfully handled internship interviews with structured, relevant answers.  
- Learned to balance technical depth with clarity for non-technical interviewers.  
- Improved overall professionalism and self-presentation during interviews.

---

## 🎓 Key Lessons Learned
Through this process, I learned that successful interviews depend on **preparation, reflection, and adaptability**.  
- Using the **STAR method** turns experiences into clear, memorable stories.  
- Research transforms interviews into genuine two-way conversations.  
- Linking certifications (PL-300 & AI-900) to real projects demonstrates both learning and application.  
- Confidence grows from preparation, not memorization — it’s about clarity, not perfection.
