## Reflection (STAR Technique)

**SITUATION:**  
As part of my career readiness and certification journey, I participated in a recorded mock interview and technical practice sessions to strengthen my communication and technical interview performance.

**TASK:**  
My goal was to improve my ability to express technical knowledge clearly, manage pressure, and communicate my achievements — such as my Power BI and Azure AI certifications — with confidence.

**ACTION:**  
- Participated in a recorded mock interview focusing on both technical and behavioral questions.  
- Completed multiple technical assessments on SQL, Power BI, and logic-based problem-solving.  
- Practiced presenting solutions step-by-step and explaining technical processes to non-technical audiences.  
- Reviewed feedback and refined my tone, pacing, and storytelling.  

**RESULT:**  
- Improved my ability to handle real interviews with confidence and structure.  
- Strengthened communication under pressure while maintaining clarity.  
- Successfully applied these skills in actual interviews — leading to positive recruiter feedback and internship opportunities.  
- Learned to bridge technical knowledge with professionalism, aligning both my confidence and competence.

---

## Key Lessons Learned
Participating in mock interviews taught me that **confidence is built through preparation and reflection**.  
I learned the importance of:
- Speaking clearly and maintaining natural body language.  
- Practicing with real questions and reviewing performance critically.  
- Using certifications and projects as conversation anchors.  
- Managing nerves through preparation and breathing techniques.  

Most importantly, I discovered that interviews are not about perfection — they’re about connection, clarity, and demonstrating growth.
