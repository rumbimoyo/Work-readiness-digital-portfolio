## 🎯 Reflection (STAR Technique)

**SITUATION:**  
As an aspiring developer and data analyst, I wanted to grow my visibility and connect with people who inspire and challenge me in the tech industry.

**TASK:**  
My goal was to create and maintain strong professional networks that could support my learning, career opportunities, and mentorship in software development, data, and AI.

**ACTION:**  
- Built and optimized my **LinkedIn** and **GitHub** profiles to reflect my skills and certifications.  
- Reached out to professionals and mentors for advice, feedback, and collaboration opportunities.  
- Attended **Women in Tech** and **Microsoft community events** in Cape Town to build genuine in-person connections.  
- Engaged in mentorship discussions focused on career growth, leadership, and inclusion in tech.  

**RESULT:**  
- Established meaningful connections with professionals who inspire me to keep learning.  
- Gained practical career advice, new perspectives, and internship opportunities.  
- Strengthened my confidence when speaking to industry leaders and presenting my work.  
- Expanded my knowledge of new tools, technologies, and workplace trends.

## 🎓 Key Lessons Learned
I learned that professional networking goes beyond collecting contacts — it’s about **building relationships that inspire growth and collaboration**.  
- Mentorship helps turn goals into actionable steps.  
- Attending tech events creates space to learn from others’ journeys.  
- Sharing my progress online allows me to encourage and connect with others who share my passion for technology.  

Networking taught me that community is one of the most valuable tools in professional success.
